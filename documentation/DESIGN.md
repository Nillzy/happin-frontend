# Happin Design System

This document defines the complete visual language for the Happin app across both light and dark themes.

---

## Brand & Style

### Light Mode — "Happin Light"

The light theme is engineered to feel like a burst of morning energy—optimistic, clear, and inherently social. It serves as a high-clarity companion for daytime exploration, moving away from the heavy immersion of dark modes toward an airy, breathable aesthetic.

The style combines **Modern Minimalism** with **Soft Tactility**. It prioritizes high-contrast legibility and expansive whitespace to reduce cognitive load during quick browsing. By utilizing a "Soft Glass" approach—where transparency is minimal but depth is conveyed through subtle layering—the interface feels lightweight yet structured. The emotional goal is to evoke a sense of "planned excitement," making event discovery feel effortless and inviting.

### Dark Mode — "Neon Horizon"

The dark theme is centered on the "Cyber-Night" aesthetic—a fusion of high-energy nightlife, electronic music culture, and futuristic technology. It targets a youthful, tech-savvy audience that values immersive experiences and social connectivity.

The design style is a sophisticated blend of **Glassmorphism** and **Retro-Vaporwave**. It utilizes deep obsidian surfaces layered with translucent, blurred materials and energized by high-chroma "glow" accents in pink and cyan. The visual language is cinematic, using large immersive hero imagery and dramatic lighting effects (drop shadows with color tints) to evoke the feeling of a live event.

---

## Colors

### Light Mode

The palette is anchored by a sophisticated **Soft White (#F8F9FA)** base, which provides a flicker-free canvas for vibrant accents. **Pure White (#FFFFFF)** is reserved exclusively for elevated surfaces like cards and modals to create a clear "layering" effect.

- **Primary (Electric Pink):** Used for main calls-to-action and critical brand moments. It drives the energetic pulse of the UI.
- **Secondary (Soft Violet):** Applied to secondary actions, category tags, and decorative gradients to soften the intensity of the pink.
- **Interactive Accents (Cyan Glow):** Utilized for active states, progress bars, and subtle highlights to provide a refreshing contrast.
- **Functional Colors:** Emerald Green and Bright Red are used sparingly for immediate feedback, ensuring high accessibility and safety.

### Dark Mode

The palette is built on a "Midnight" foundation, using `#12131A` as the base background to allow vibrant accents to pop.

- **Primary (Neon Pink):** Used for branding, critical actions, and high-energy status indicators.
- **Secondary (Vivid Violet):** Employed for supporting elements and soft background fills.
- **Tertiary (Electric Cyan):** Used for information links, success states, and "high-tech" secondary accents.
- **Neutral (Zinc/Obsidian):** Grayscale values are shifted toward cool blues and purples to maintain the nocturnal atmosphere.

The system utilizes "Glow States" where key elements emit a colored shadow or drop-shadow (using `rgba` values of the primary and tertiary colors) to simulate neon lighting.

---

## Typography

### Light Mode

The light theme employs a dual-font strategy to balance personality with utility. **Plus Jakarta Sans** is used for headlines to provide a friendly, rounded geometric feel that aligns with the "Happin" brand. **Inter** is utilized for body text and labels to ensure maximum readability and a clean, systematic appearance.

Text hierarchy is strictly maintained through weight and scale. Headlines use heavy weights and slight negative letter spacing for a punchy, editorial look. Body text prioritizes generous line heights to facilitate easy scanning of event details.

### Dark Mode

The dark theme utilizes **Inter** across all levels for its technical clarity and modern feel.

- **Brand Expression:** The primary logo and key headlines use "Display" weights (ExtraBold/Black) with tight tracking and occasional italics to suggest movement and energy.
- **Hierarchy:** Strong contrast between headline weights (700+) and body text (400) is essential.
- **Utility:** Small labels and tags use a slightly increased letter spacing and a Semibold (600) weight to ensure legibility against dark, vibrant backgrounds.

---

## Layout & Spacing

### Light Mode

The layout philosophy follows a **12-column Fluid Grid** for desktop and a **4-column Fluid Grid** for mobile. A strict 8px spatial system ensures consistency across all components.

Margins are generous (24px - 32px on mobile) to create a "breathable" atmosphere. The system favors vertical stacking for event listings, using the `lg` spacing unit to separate distinct content blocks. Grouped elements, such as metadata within a card, utilize the `sm` and `xs` units to maintain visual proximity.

### Dark Mode

The system follows a **Fixed-Width Centered Grid** for desktop (max-width 1024px) and a **Fluid Grid** for mobile.

- **Margins:** A standard 24px (`grid_margin`) is used for external page padding.
- **Gutters:** 16px (`grid_gutter`) separates primary content cards.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px base unit. Component internal padding typically uses `md` (16px) or `lg` (24px) to ensure airiness within dense, high-contrast UI elements.

---

## Elevation & Depth

### Light Mode

Hierarchy is established through **Ambient Shadows** rather than harsh borders. Because the background is a soft gray-white, shadows should use a subtle cool-tinted blur to feel natural.

- **Level 0 (Base):** Soft White (#F8F9FA) background.
- **Level 1 (Cards/Inputs):** Pure White (#FFFFFF) with a 2px stroke of #000000 at 5% opacity and a soft shadow (0px 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)).
- **Level 2 (Interactive/Floating):** Pure White (#FFFFFF) with a more pronounced shadow (0px 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)).
- **Interactive State:** When an element is hovered, the elevation increases slightly, and a subtle Cyan Glow (#00D1FF) outer glow may be applied to signify focus.

### Dark Mode

Depth is created through a mix of **Tonal Layering** and **Glassmorphism**:

1. **Level 0 (Base):** Deep black/neutral-dark background (`#12131A`).
2. **Level 1 (Cards):** Surface containers (`#1E1F26`) with a subtle `white/5` border to define edges without heavy shadows.
3. **Level 2 (Active/RSVP Cards):** Elevated containers with higher contrast (`#292931`) and colored glow shadows (e.g., `rgba(255, 92, 138, 0.05)`).
4. **Level 3 (Navigation):** Translucent overlays (`bg-zinc-950/70`) with high-density backdrop blur (20px+) and bottom borders to separate the header from the content.

---

## Shapes

Both themes share a **Large Rounded** shape language to reinforce a friendly and approachable personality. Sharp corners are avoided entirely.

- **Small Components (Tags, Badges):** 8px radius.
- **Medium Components (Buttons, Inputs):** 12px - 16px radius.
- **Large Components (Cards, Containers):** 20px radius.
- **Full Pill:** Used for category filters, primary search bars, and avatars (9999px).

---

## Components

### Buttons

| Variant | Light Mode | Dark Mode |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Primary | Electric Pink with white text, subtle top-to-bottom gradient (5% shift) | Solid `primary-container` fill, bold text, matching color glow shadow |
| Secondary | Soft Violet outline or light tint | Thin tertiary borders with subtle background tints on hover |

All buttons require a minimum height of 48px to ensure touch-friendly use.

### Cards

Event cards are the centerpiece of the system. They use Level 1 elevation with a 20px radius. Imagery within cards should have a 16px internal radius, creating a nested "frame" effect that feels premium and contained.

In dark mode, cards additionally feature a "top-border" gradient accent (Primary to Tertiary) for high-priority user tasks.

### Input Fields

Inputs use a solid background with a subtle border. Upon focus, the border transitions to Cyan Glow with a 1px solid stroke. In light mode, inputs are Pure White with a Soft White border and mid-gray placeholders (#A0AEC0). In dark mode, inputs follow the surface-container tonal layering.

### Chips & Tags

Used for event categories (e.g., "Music," "Art"). In light mode, these have a light secondary tint background with darker text. In dark mode, semi-transparent backgrounds (`secondary/20`) with matching text and icons.

### Floating Action Button (FAB)

For creating new events or quick search, use a circular FAB in Electric Pink, positioned in the bottom right, utilizing Level 2 elevation for maximum visibility.

### Top App Bar (Dark Mode)

Docked at the top with heavy backdrop-blur (XL) and high-transparency fill to allow content to bleed through while maintaining legibility.

### Widgets (Dark Mode)

Small, centered utility cards (1:1 aspect ratio) used for data points like weather or countdowns, using icons as primary visual anchors.
